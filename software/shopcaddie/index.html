How It Works

Three steps. Zero extra work.

01

Upload Your Customer List

Hand us your paper log, spreadsheet, or POS export. We digitize it and build your customer database — names, phone numbers, purchase history, all of it.

02

AI Writes the Messages

ShopCaddie crafts personalized texts based on what each customer bought and when. Post-purchase check-ins, review requests, win-back messages — all in your shop's voice.

03

Approve and Send

Preview every message before it goes out, or flip on auto-send and let ShopCaddie handle it. You stay on the shop floor. Your customers stay engaged.

Features

Everything a marketing employee would do — without the payroll.

Post-Purchase Follow-Ups

Your customers hear from you days after buying new clubs — a personal check-in that feels like it came from the owner, not a robot.

Google Review Requests

Automatically ask happy customers to leave a review at the perfect moment. More reviews means more foot traffic from "golf shop near me" searches.

Lapsed Customer Re-Engagement

Haven't seen someone in six months? ShopCaddie notices and sends a personalized message to bring them back in.

Seasonal Campaigns

Spring driver launches, holiday gift guides, demo day promos — pre-built campaigns that go out at the right time, personalized to each customer's history.

QR Code Lead Capture

A card at your counter. Customers scan, enter their info, and they're in the system. Replaces the paper log entirely.

AI Social Content Coming Soon

Instagram and Facebook posts drafted from your inventory and local context. Approve with one tap or auto-publish.
