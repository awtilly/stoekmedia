What We Do

Comprehensive Security Solutions

Enterprise-grade protection with the accountability of a local partner. Every service backed by data you can see.

Core Service

Security Guard Services

Professionally trained, uniformed officers stationed at your facility around the clock. Every officer is background-checked, state-licensed, and equipped with real-time reporting tools. We staff commercial buildings, warehouses, healthcare facilities, government properties, and private events.

Patrol

Mobile Patrol

GPS-tracked vehicles running scheduled and randomized routes across your properties. Visible deterrence backed by documented patrol logs and real-time alerts. Cover multiple sites efficiently.

Access

Ingress & Egress Control

Checkpoint security for facilities, construction sites, and events. Credential verification, visitor management, and secure perimeter enforcement.

Technology

Performance Monitoring

Proprietary KPI-tracking platform gives you full operational transparency. Shift coverage, patrol completions, response times, and officer metrics — all in real time.
