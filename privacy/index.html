Privacy

What stoekmedia.com collects when you visit, why, and how to opt out.

Last updated November 13, 2026

Stoek Media is run by Joe Stoehner in Charlotte, North Carolina. This page covers the Stoek Media pages on stoekmedia.com: the home page, About, Blog, Software and Contact. The products have their own policies, linked at the bottom.

Site statistics (Google Analytics)

These pages use Google Analytics 4 to count visits. It records which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are (country and city, worked out from the IP address, which Google Analytics does not store), what kind of device and browser was used, and which site or search sent the visitor here.

To tell one visit from another, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_B30VB9NW4P, which last up to two years.

The statistics are used for one thing: to see which pages get read. They are not used for advertising, they are not sold, and the advertising features of Google Analytics are switched off. Visit-level data is kept for up to 14 months.

Google processes this data on our behalf. Google's own explanation is at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in Europe and the UK

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the analytics cookies above are not set. Google Analytics receives a cookieless page count only, which cannot be tied to you from one page to the next.

How to opt out

  • Install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on for your browser.
  • Block or clear cookies for stoekmedia.com in your browser settings.
  • Use a content blocker. The site works the same without analytics.

Fonts and hosting

The typefaces on these pages load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers and Google receives your IP address when it does. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you request a page and may keep it in server logs for security. See Google Fonts and privacy and GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

There are no forms on these pages. If you email joe@stoekmedia.com, your message and address are kept so we can reply, and are not added to any mailing list.

The products

GreenDoor and Madness Draft are covered by their own policies, and their pages do not use Google Analytics: GreenDoor privacy policy, Madness Draft privacy policy.

Questions

Email joe@stoekmedia.com. If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
