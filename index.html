A real estate CRM built around how one agent actually works a day: clients in, listings matched, showings scheduled, documents signed, follow-ups sent. One codebase, no plugins, no per-seat upsell.

  • Client records with full create, read, update, delete
  • Listing-to-client matching engine
  • Showing scheduling
  • BoldSign e-signature, in-app
  • AI assistant chatbot with voice input
  • AI-generated daily briefing
  • SendGrid transactional and outreach email
  • Admin panel with role-based access
