Help & Support

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Need a hand? Email support@stoekmedia.com and include your room code and the name you used in the room. We answer within a few days, faster during the tournament.

The Privacy Policy explains what the app stores and how to have it removed.

Madness Draft is a free app for running a snake-style team draft and bracket pool with friends during the college basketball tournament. One person (the commissioner) creates a room, everyone else joins it with the room code, the group drafts the 64 teams in snake order, and the leaderboard updates itself as games go final. It runs in any modern browser at stoekmedia.com/madness, can be added to your phone's Home Screen, and is also available as an iPhone app. All three show the same room.

1. Getting started

1.1 Creating a room (commissioner)

  1. Open the app and type a room name under Create a Room. The name becomes the room code: letters and numbers only, 2 to 20 characters, shown in capitals. Pick something your group will not guess wrong, such as SMITHPOOL27.
  2. Set a 4-digit admin PIN. This PIN unlocks every room-wide setting, so do not share it with the players.
  3. Open Settings (the gear) and import the teams under Team Management. Import from NCAA.com loads the field automatically once the bracket is announced on Selection Sunday. Before that you can paste a list (one team per line as Team, Seed, Region) or add teams one at a time.
  4. Still in Settings, choose the Scoring preset, whether the Upset Bonus is on, and the Draft Clock (no limit, 60, 90 or 120 seconds per pick).
  5. Share the room with Share invite link on the Draft tab, or by tapping the room code at the top of the screen. The link lets people straight in. Anyone who types the room code by hand instead is also asked for the room's 6-character invite code, shown next to that button, so send it along if you only tell people the room code. New invite code replaces it: links you sent earlier stop working for new players, and everyone already in the room is unaffected. Players add themselves; you can also type names into the player list on the Draft tab for people who have not arrived yet. The order of the list is the first-round draft order; it snakes back for the second round and so on. You can join as a player yourself with Join as Player (Commissioner).
  6. When everyone is in, press Start Draft. It needs at least two players and the teams loaded.

1.2 Joining a room (players)

  1. Open the link the commissioner sent. On an iPhone with the app installed the link opens the app. Or open the app and enter the room code under Join a Room; you will then also be asked for the invite code the commissioner gave you.
  2. Under Join as New Player, enter your name and choose a 4-digit player PIN. If the commissioner already put your name in the list, tap it under Already in this pool? instead and set your PIN there.
  3. The PIN protects your spot so nobody else in the room can pick for you. Remember it: we cannot look it up.

The app remembers which player you are on that phone, so you only do this once per device. Rooms you have been in appear under Your Tournaments on the start screen.

1.3 Installing on your phone

iPhone and iPad. In Safari, tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen, or install the Madness Draft app from the App Store. Notifications on iPhone only work from the Home Screen version or the App Store app, not from a Safari tab.

Android and desktop. Chrome and Edge show an Install banner the first time you open a room. Dismiss it and the app keeps working in the browser.

2. The draft

  • Order. Snake order: 1, 2, 3 … then back 3, 2, 1, then 1, 2, 3 again, until all 64 teams are gone. The Draft tab always shows who is on the clock and who is next.
  • Your turn. Tap a team in the list to pick it. Teams already taken drop out of the list; the count above it shows how many are left, and the Rosters panel shows who has what. If notifications are on, your phone buzzes when it is your turn.
  • Draft clock. If the commissioner set a time limit, the clock counts down on everyone's screen. When it runs out the app picks the highest remaining seed for you, so you are never skipped.
  • Mistakes. The commissioner can Undo Last Pick or Reset Draft to start over with the same players and teams.
  • Everyone sees the same thing. Picks sync in real time. If two people pick at once, the first one to reach the server wins and the other sees "Team already picked"; just pick again.

3. Scoring

Each win earns the team's owner the points for that round. The commissioner chooses the values in Settings; changes apply to the whole room immediately.

Doubling1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32Classic bracket-pool weighting; the champion's owner usually wins.
Linear1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6Early upsets matter more; the race stays open longer.
Back-loaded1, 2, 4, 8, 20, 40Almost everything rides on the Final Four.
Flat1 per roundCount the wins.

Any round's value can be edited by hand. The Upset Bonus adds the seed difference when a lower seed wins (a 12 beating a 5 is worth 7 extra). The Leaderboard tab shows totals, points by round, and each player's teams with how many are still alive.

4. The bracket and live scores

  • Scores tab. During the tournament it lists the day's games with live scores, refreshing about once a minute. Scores come from NCAA.com through our own relay, so your phone never talks to NCAA.com directly.
  • Results reach the bracket automatically while someone in the room has the Scores tab open. The next time anyone opens the room the bracket, leaderboard and eliminated teams are already up to date.
  • Fixing a result. The commissioner can tap a team on the Bracket tab to advance it by hand and press and hold a game to clear it. This is also how a room without live scores is kept current.
  • First Four. Play-in games are settled by the app once they go final. If a slot still shows both teams, the commissioner can use Check Play-in Results in Settings after the games.

5. Notifications

The first time you open a room the app offers to turn notifications on. Say yes and you will be told when it is your turn to pick, when one of your teams' games goes final, and when the lead changes. Nothing else is ever sent.

  • Not getting them? On iPhone, make sure you opened the room from the Home Screen icon or the App Store app, and that notifications are allowed for it in iOS Settings. In a browser, check the site's notification permission in the address bar. Turning notifications off and on again creates a fresh token.
  • Too many? Turn them off in your phone's settings for the app or the site. The room keeps working without them.

6. PINs and access

  • Forgot your player PIN? Ask the commissioner. They can remove your spot and add it again, after which you set a new PIN. Your picks stay with the spot.
  • Forgot the admin PIN? Email us with the room code from the device that created the room and we will help you recover access. There is no self-service reset, on purpose: the admin PIN is the only thing protecting the room's settings.
  • Wrong PIN entered several times? The app slows each further attempt down for a few seconds. Wait and try again; nothing is locked permanently.
  • The commissioner's unlock lasts four hours on that device, then the admin PIN is asked for again.
  • Who can see what. Anyone with the room code can view the room; joining it as a new player also takes the invite link or invite code. Only a spot's PIN can change its picks, and only the admin PIN can change room settings. Treat the code like an invitation.

7. Something looks wrong

  • The room shows an old state. Close and reopen the app. The app keeps an offline copy of the room so it opens without a connection, and that copy can briefly lag the live room.
  • "No games found" on the Scores tab. Either there are no tournament games today or the score feed had a bad minute. It retries on its own; the bracket and leaderboard are unaffected.
  • "Tournament bracket not available yet." NCAA.com publishes the field on Selection Sunday evening. Import again after that, or paste the teams in by hand.
  • A team's name or seed is wrong. Before the draft starts the commissioner can fix it under Team Management: re-import from NCAA.com, or Clear All Teams and add the corrected list by hand.
  • Lost connection during the draft. Picks made while offline are saved on the phone and sent as soon as it reconnects. If somebody else picked the same team in the meantime the app tells you and lets you pick again.

8. Starting over and deleting data

New Tournament at the bottom of Settings deletes the room's players, picks and bracket so the same room code can be used next year. It cannot be undone. To remove just one player, the commissioner uses the remove button next to the name on the Draft tab (before the draft starts). To have a whole room or your own name deleted by us, email support@stoekmedia.com with the room code; see the Privacy Policy for details.

9. Contact

Email support@stoekmedia.com. Include the room code, the name you used, what you expected and what happened instead, and the device and browser or app version if you know them. Screenshots help.
