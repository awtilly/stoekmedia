Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what Madness Draft ("the app") stores, where that information goes, and how to have it removed. Madness Draft is a free app for running a snake-style team draft and bracket pool with friends during the college basketball tournament. It is operated by Stoek Media LLC ("we," "us") and is available on the web at stoekmedia.com/madness and as a mobile app. The same policy applies to both.

The short version: there are no accounts, no email addresses, no advertising and no analytics. The app stores the display name you type in, a scrambled copy of your 4-digit PIN, your picks, and, only if you turn notifications on, a push-notification token. Everything lives in the room you joined and goes away when the room's commissioner resets it.

1. Information the app stores

1.1 What you type in

  • Display name. The name you or the commissioner enter for your spot in the room. Use a nickname if you prefer; nothing requires a real name.
  • 4-digit PIN. Players and commissioners can protect their spot with a 4-digit PIN. The PIN itself is never stored. The app stores a salted SHA-256 hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the PIN.
  • Room code. The short code that identifies your group's room.
  • Your picks. The teams you draft, your bracket selections, and the room settings the commissioner chooses (scoring system, draft order, team list).

1.2 Push notification token

If you choose to allow notifications, your browser or phone issues a push token through Firebase Cloud Messaging. The app stores that token inside your room so it can tell you when it is your turn to pick, when a game goes final, and when the leaderboard changes. The token identifies a device, not a person. Decline the notification prompt and no token is ever created. Tokens that stop working are removed automatically.

1.3 Stored on your device only

The app keeps a small amount of information in your browser's local storage so it works offline and remembers you between visits: the rooms you have joined recently, which player in each room is you, whether you dismissed the install or notification banner, and a cached copy of the room so it can open without a connection. This never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

1.4 What we do not collect

No email address, no phone number, no password, no payment details, no location, no contacts, no photos. The app does not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts, and it does not use cookies.

2. How the information is used

  • To run your room: show everyone the same draft, bracket, and leaderboard in real time.
  • To check that the person editing a spot is the person who set its PIN.
  • To send the notifications you opted into.
  • To show live scores during the tournament.

We do not sell this information, share it with marketers, or use it for anything other than running the app. There is no real-money play: the app handles no payments, entry fees or prizes.

4. Who can see your room

Anyone who knows your room code can open the room and see the display names, picks, bracket and leaderboard in it. Treat the code like an invitation: share it with your group, not publicly. PIN-protected spots can be viewed by everyone in the room but edited only by someone with the PIN. Room-wide settings can be changed only with the commissioner's PIN.

5. Security

Connections use TLS. PINs are stored only as salted hashes. Repeated wrong PIN attempts are slowed down. Data at rest in Firebase is encrypted by Google. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not reuse a PIN you use anywhere else, and tell us promptly if you believe a room has been tampered with.

6. Retention and deletion

A room and everything in it stays until its commissioner chooses "New Tournament" in Settings, which deletes the room's players, picks and bracket. Push tokens are removed automatically when they stop working, and a commissioner can clear them by emailing us with the room code.

To have your name, PIN hash or picks removed from a room, ask the room's commissioner, who can delete your spot. If you would rather contact us directly, or want an entire room deleted, email support@stoekmedia.com with the room code. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Because the app holds only a display name and your picks, the quickest route is usually to ask your commissioner or email support@stoekmedia.com with the room code and the name you used.

8. Children

Madness Draft is meant for adults organising a pool with friends, family or coworkers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has entered a name in a room, email us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Because the app has no accounts or email addresses, this page is the only place changes are announced, so check it if you have questions.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email support@stoekmedia.com.
