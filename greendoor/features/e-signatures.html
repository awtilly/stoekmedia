E-Signatures

Built-In Electronic Signatures for Real Estate

Send documents for electronic signature directly from a client's file in GreenDoor. Powered by BoldSign, with two flexible signing workflows, webhook notifications, and automatic download of signed PDFs. No separate e-signature subscription required.

Try GreenDoor See How It Works
Two Workflows

Choose How You Place Signature Fields

GreenDoor gives you two ways to prepare documents for signing, depending on how much control you need over field placement.

1

Auto-Place Fields

Upload your document and GreenDoor automatically places signature, date, and initial fields based on common real estate document layouts. Add signers with their name and email, set a signing order if needed, and send. Best for standard contracts and disclosures where you just need the basics signed quickly.

2

Embedded Drag-and-Drop Editor

For documents that need precise field placement, GreenDoor opens a full embedded editor powered by BoldSign. Drag and drop signature fields, text fields, checkboxes, date fields, and initials anywhere on the document. Assign each field to a specific signer. Preview the document exactly as your client will see it before sending. Ideal for custom agreements, addendums, and non-standard forms.

Client Integration

Send Documents Directly from a Client's Page

E-signatures in GreenDoor aren't a separate module you have to navigate to. You send documents from the Files tab on a client's detail page, right where you already manage their contracts and disclosures.

Select a document from the client's uploaded files (or upload a new one), choose your signing workflow, add signers, and send. The envelope status appears in the client's activity feed and file list so you never lose track of what's been sent, what's pending, and what's completed.

Notifications

Webhook Notifications When Documents Are Signed

GreenDoor uses BoldSign's webhook system to get real-time notifications when a signer views, signs, or declines a document. As soon as all parties have signed, GreenDoor automatically updates the envelope status in your CRM.

You'll see a notification in your activity feed the moment a document is completed, so you can take the next step immediately — whether that's submitting an offer, scheduling an inspection, or moving the client to the next stage.

Auto-Download

Signed PDFs Saved to the Client's File Automatically

When a document is fully signed, GreenDoor automatically downloads the completed PDF and saves it to the client's Files tab. No manual downloading, no hunting through email for the final version, no re-uploading to your CRM.

Every signed document is stored alongside the client's other files — contracts, pre-approval letters, inspection reports — so everything lives in one place. You can download or share any completed document at any time.

Templates

Document Templates for Repeat Workflows

For documents you send regularly — seller disclosures, buyer agency agreements, inspection contingency forms — GreenDoor lets you save document templates with pre-configured field placements and signer roles.

When you need to send one, select the template, assign your signers, and send. The fields are already placed where they need to be. This saves significant time for agents who handle high volumes of similar transactions.

Included in GreenDoor

No Separate E-Signature Subscription

Most real estate CRMs either don't include e-signatures at all, or charge $20-$40/month extra for a basic integration that sends you to a third-party platform. GreenDoor's e-signature functionality is built into the CRM itself and included in your plan.

You get the full signing experience — multiple signers, field placement, status tracking, webhook notifications, and auto-download — without paying for DocuSign, Dotloop, or any other separate signing service.

BoldSign powers the signing infrastructure behind the scenes, so you get enterprise-grade security and legal compliance. Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, and every signature includes a full audit trail with timestamps and IP addresses.
