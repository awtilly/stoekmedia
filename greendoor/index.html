Features

Everything you need to close deals.

👥

Client Management

Full profiles with budget, preferences, timeline, pre-approval status, and notes. Track every client from first contact to closing day.

🏠

Listings & Smart Matching

Add listings manually or paste a URL to auto-import details with AI. Match properties to clients with intelligent compatibility scoring.

📅

Showings & Calendar

Schedule property showings, manage your full calendar with month and week views, and capture client ratings and feedback after each visit.

✍

E-Signatures

Send documents for electronic signature directly from client files. Add multiple signers, place fields visually, and track status in real time.

✉

Email & Documents

Send emails with templates and merge tags. Upload and organize contracts, disclosures, and inspections. Share files with clients in one click.

✨

AI Assistant

Type or speak to your AI assistant. Draft emails, summarize clients, schedule showings, set follow-ups, and get intelligent next-step suggestions.

How It Works

Up and running in minutes.

No complex setup. No training required. Just log in and start managing your clients.

1

Get Your Account

Your brokerage admin sets up your agent account. You get an email, set your password, and you're in.

2

3

Close More Deals

Match listings, send contracts for signature, and let AI help you stay on top of every client relationship.
