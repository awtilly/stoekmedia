Why GreenDoor

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Most CRMs are bloated, expensive, and built for enterprise teams. GreenDoor is different — purpose-built for individual agents and small brokerages who want to stay organized without the overhead.

Track every client from first contact to closing day. Log calls, manage documents, compare properties, and never let a lead slip through the cracks.

Features

👤

Client Management

Track every client with detailed profiles — budget, preferences, timeline, pre-approval status, and free-form notes. Never lose context on a deal.

📅

Activity Tracking

Log calls, emails, notes, and showings. A complete timeline for every client so you always know where things stand.

Document Storage
