FAQ

Common questions.

What is GreenDoor CRM?

GreenDoor is a real estate CRM built specifically for how agents actually work. It includes AI-powered client insights, electronic document signatures, smart listing matching, showing and calendar management, and automated follow-up emails — all in one platform.

How is GreenDoor different from other real estate CRMs?

GreenDoor was built from real agent feedback, not top-down assumptions. It solves specific workflow pain points like intelligent listing-to-client matching and built-in e-signatures — features that most CRMs either lack or charge extra for. AI isn't bolted on; it's the foundation.

Does GreenDoor integrate with MLS?

GreenDoor supports manual listing entry with address autocomplete out of the box. Automated MLS integration via SimplyRETS is available as an add-on for agents who want real-time listing synchronization and automated match scoring.

How much does GreenDoor CRM cost?

GreenDoor offers three plans: Starter at $29/month for solo agents, Professional at $59/month with AI assistant and e-signatures, and Team at $99/month for brokerages. All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Does GreenDoor have electronic signatures?

Yes. GreenDoor has built-in e-signature functionality powered by BoldSign, so agents can send, track, and manage document signatures directly within the CRM without paying for a separate e-signature service.

Can the AI assistant help with client follow-ups?

Yes. GreenDoor's AI assistant can draft personalized follow-up emails, suggest optimal contact timing based on client activity, and help prioritize your pipeline so you focus on the hottest leads first.
