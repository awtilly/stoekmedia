Your account is set up. Here's how to get started.

1
Add your first client

From your dashboard, just type "Add Sarah Smith as a buyer with email sarah@example.com" — Sage will set them up. Or visit Clients and add manually.

2
Add a listing

From the dashboard, paste a Zillow or Realtor.com URL into Sage and say "Add this listing." Or go to Listings and add one manually.

3
Upload document templates

Open the Templates page from the sidebar to upload your go-to contracts and disclosures. Sage will help map signature fields automatically.
