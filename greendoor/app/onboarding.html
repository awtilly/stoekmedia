What You Can Do

GreenDoor is built for how realtors actually work. Here's what's at your fingertips.

Client Management

Track leads, buyers, and sellers with full profiles.

  • Contact details, budgets, timelines, and preferences
  • Activity log tracks every interaction automatically
  • Quick filters to sort by status, budget, or date
Listings & Matching

Add listings and match properties to clients.

  • Paste a URL to auto-import listing details
  • Smart scoring matches properties to client preferences
  • Bookmark and organize favorites per client
Showings & Calendar

Schedule showings and manage your calendar.

  • Month and week views with drag-and-drop scheduling
  • Collect client feedback and star ratings after showings
  • Automatic reminders so nothing slips through
Documents & E-Signatures

Upload, template, and send for signature.

  • Create reusable templates for contracts and disclosures
  • Electronic signatures with full audit trail
  • Organize files per client with drag-and-drop upload
Email & Communication

Send emails with templates and merge tags.

  • Send directly from each client's page
  • Merge tags auto-fill client and property details
  • Full activity log tracks opens and replies
AI Assistant

Draft emails, summarize clients, and more.

  • Type or speak commands to your AI assistant
  • Create clients, schedule showings, and draft emails hands-free
  • Get intelligent suggestions based on client data

3
Upload document templates

Head to Settings to upload your go-to contracts and disclosures for quick signature workflows.
