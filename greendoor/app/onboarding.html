What You Can Do

GreenDoor is built for how realtors actually work. Here's what's at your fingertips.

👥
Client Management

Track leads, buyers, and sellers with full contact details, preferences, budgets, and timeline.

🏠
Listings & Matching

Add listings manually or paste a URL to auto-import details. Match properties to clients with smart scoring.

📅
Showings & Calendar

Schedule property showings, manage your calendar, and track client feedback with ratings.

✍
Documents & E-Signatures

Upload files, create reusable templates, and send documents for electronic signature — all in one place.

✉
Email & Communication

Send emails directly from each client's page with templates, merge tags, and full activity tracking.

✨
AI Assistant

Type or speak to your AI assistant. Draft emails, summarize clients, schedule showings, and get intelligent suggestions.
