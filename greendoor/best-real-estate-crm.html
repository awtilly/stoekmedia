What to Look for in a Real Estate CRM

Before you commit, run through this checklist. The right CRM depends on your business, not a feature count.

  • Does it match your workflow? A CRM should fit how you already work, not force you to change everything. If you spend most of your day on showings, you need showing management. If you rely on email, you need great automation. Try before you buy.
  • What's the true monthly cost? Add up the CRM subscription plus any essential add-ons (e-signatures, dialer, showing tools). A $25/month CRM that requires $50/month in add-ons isn't really $25/month.
  • How fast can you get started? If onboarding takes weeks and requires training videos, that's time you're not selling. Look for CRMs with fast setup and intuitive interfaces.
  • Will it grow with you? Starting solo but plan to build a team? Make sure the CRM has team features you can grow into without migrating to a new platform.
  • How is the support? When something breaks at 9 PM before a morning closing, can you get help? Check support hours, channels, and response times.
  • Can you get your data out? CRM lock-in is real. Make sure the platform offers data export so you're not trapped if you need to switch.

Common Mistakes Agents Make Choosing a CRM

  1. Buying more CRM than you need. A solo agent doesn't need a platform built for 50-person brokerages. You'll pay for features you never use and spend weeks learning an interface designed for someone else. Start with what fits your business today.
  2. Ignoring the total cost of ownership. The CRM subscription is just the starting line. Factor in e-signature tools, showing management apps, dialer services, and any integrations that carry their own fees. A "cheap" CRM can quietly become the most expensive option.
  3. Choosing based on brand name alone. The most popular CRM isn't always the best fit for your workflow. A smaller, more focused platform that nails your specific needs will outperform a big-name tool you only half-use. Always do a free trial.
  4. Not testing with real data. A demo with fake contacts tells you nothing. During your free trial, import your actual client list, set up your real workflows, and use the CRM for day-to-day tasks. That's the only way to know if it fits.
