**Description:**
GreenDoor is the CRM built for working realtors — everything you juggle in a day,
behind one door.

CLIENTS THAT NEVER SLIP
Track every buyer and seller from first lead to closing day. Statuses, budgets,
timelines, notes, and a full activity history — searchable in seconds.

LISTINGS WITH BUYER MATCHING
Your inventory with photos, specs, and price history. Quick Match instantly surfaces
which of your clients fit which listing.

A CALENDAR THAT WORKS LIKE YOU DO
Showings, follow-ups, and open houses in one view — with ShowingTime import and a feed
you can subscribe to from Google Calendar or Outlook.

SAGE, YOUR AI ASSISTANT
Ask in plain English — or just talk. "Who haven't I contacted this month?" "Summarize my
pipeline." Sage answers from your actual data.

A MARKETING STUDIO IN YOUR POCKET
Import a listing and design social posts, open house announcements, flyers, and postcards
in minutes. Your name, your brand, print-ready — then publish straight to the share sheet.

TEMPLATES AND E-SIGNATURES
Upload the PDFs you use every day. GreenDoor auto-fills client and listing data and
routes them for signature.

Works offline in the field and syncs when you're back. Your data stays yours — export or
delete your account anytime.
