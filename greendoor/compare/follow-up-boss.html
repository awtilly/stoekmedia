GreenDoor CRM vs Follow Up Boss — Which Real Estate CRM Is Right for You?

Both GreenDoor and Follow Up Boss help real estate agents manage clients and close deals. But they take very different approaches. Here's an honest, side-by-side breakdown to help you decide.

Where GreenDoor Wins

Built-in E-Signatures

GreenDoor includes electronic signatures powered by BoldSign — no separate subscription to DocuSign or DotLoop needed. Send contracts, disclosures, and agreements for signature directly from the CRM. Follow Up Boss doesn't offer e-signatures at all, so you'll need to pay for and manage a separate tool.

AI-Powered Assistant

GreenDoor's AI assistant does more than auto-responses. It drafts personalized emails, summarizes client history, schedules showings through natural conversation, and prioritizes your pipeline. It understands real estate workflows and can take actions directly in the CRM — like creating follow-ups or logging activities.

Smart Listing Matching

Enter a client's preferences (budget, location, beds, baths, features) and GreenDoor automatically scores every listing against those criteria. A 7-factor match algorithm surfaces the best properties so you spend less time searching and more time showing. Follow Up Boss doesn't offer listing matching.

Showing Management

Schedule showings, track which properties each client has seen, collect feedback and ratings, and manage your showing calendar — all inside the CRM. Follow Up Boss requires separate tools for showing coordination.

Lower Price

GreenDoor starts at $29/month. When you factor in the e-signature subscription you'd need separately with Follow Up Boss ($20-30/month), GreenDoor delivers more functionality for significantly less.

Where Follow Up Boss Wins

Established Ecosystem

Follow Up Boss has been around since 2011 and has 250+ integrations with lead sources (Zillow, Realtor.com, etc.), marketing tools, and other real estate platforms. If your workflow depends on connecting many third-party tools, FUB's integration library is hard to beat.

Automated Lead Routing

For teams, Follow Up Boss excels at automatically distributing incoming leads based on rules (round-robin, zip code, source, etc.). GreenDoor supports team workflows, but lead routing is currently manual via the admin panel.

Brand Recognition

Follow Up Boss is a well-known name in real estate tech. If you value community support, training resources, and a large user base, FUB has the edge of a decade in the market.

Which One Is Right for You?

Choose GreenDoor if you…

  • Want e-signatures, AI, and showing management in one tool
  • Are a solo agent or small team looking for an all-in-one CRM
  • Want to match listings to client preferences automatically
  • Prefer a simpler interface without enterprise complexity
  • Want to spend less on your tech stack

Choose Follow Up Boss if you…

  • Run a large team and need automated lead routing
  • Rely on 10+ integrations with lead sources and marketing tools
  • Already have separate tools for e-signatures and showings
  • Value an established platform with a large community
