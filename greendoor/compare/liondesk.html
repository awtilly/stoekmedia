GreenDoor CRM vs LionDesk — Modern CRM for Today's Agent

LionDesk has been a popular choice for real estate agents since 2014, especially for its video email and texting features. GreenDoor takes a different approach — built-in AI, e-signatures, and showing management in one modern platform. Here's the honest breakdown.

Where GreenDoor Wins

Built-in E-Signatures

This is a major gap in LionDesk. GreenDoor includes electronic signatures powered by BoldSign on every plan — send contracts, disclosures, and agreements for signature directly from client records. LionDesk doesn't offer e-signatures at all, meaning you'll need a separate DocuSign or DotLoop subscription ($20–30/month extra) and you'll be switching between tools constantly.

More Capable AI

GreenDoor's AI assistant goes far beyond auto-responses. It drafts personalized emails based on client history, summarizes timelines, schedules showings through natural conversation, prioritizes your pipeline, and takes actions directly in the CRM. LionDesk's AI features are limited to basic lead follow-up and automated text responses — helpful, but not in the same category.

Showing Management

Schedule showings, track which properties clients have visited, collect feedback and ratings, and manage your entire showing calendar inside the CRM. LionDesk doesn't include any showing management tools, so you'll need yet another app (ShowingTime, Calendly, etc.) to coordinate property tours.

Smart Listing Matching

Enter a client's preferences — budget, location, beds, baths, must-haves — and GreenDoor's 7-factor matching algorithm automatically scores every listing. You spend less time searching and more time showing the right properties. LionDesk doesn't offer listing matching.

Modern Interface

GreenDoor was built in 2025 with a clean, modern UI that feels like the tools you use every day. LionDesk's interface, while functional, shows its age in places — navigation can feel cluttered, and some workflows take more clicks than they should.

Where LionDesk Wins

Video Email & Video Texting

LionDesk's standout feature is built-in video messaging. Record and send video emails and video texts directly from the CRM — no third-party tools needed. For agents who use video as a core part of their communication strategy, this is genuinely useful and something GreenDoor doesn't currently offer.

Power Dialer

LionDesk includes a built-in power dialer for high-volume prospecting. If cold calling or circle prospecting is a key part of your lead generation, having the dialer inside your CRM with automatic contact logging is a real time-saver.

Transaction Management

LionDesk offers built-in transaction management to track deals from contract to close. While GreenDoor handles the e-signature side of transactions, LionDesk provides a more complete transaction pipeline view with milestones, checklists, and status tracking.

More Established

LionDesk has been serving real estate agents since 2014. It has a larger user base, more online training resources, and a proven track record. If you prefer a platform with years of community feedback baked in, LionDesk has the edge in maturity.

Which One Is Right for You?

Choose GreenDoor if you…

  • Want e-signatures built into your CRM (no extra subscription)
  • Value a powerful AI assistant that drafts emails, schedules showings, and prioritizes leads
  • Need showing management and listing matching in one tool
  • Prefer a modern, clean interface that's fast to learn
  • Want to consolidate your tech stack into fewer tools

Choose LionDesk if you…

  • Use video email or video texting as a core communication strategy
  • Do high-volume cold calling and need a built-in power dialer
  • Want built-in transaction management with pipeline tracking
  • Already have separate e-signature and showing tools you're happy with
  • Prefer a more established platform with a larger community
